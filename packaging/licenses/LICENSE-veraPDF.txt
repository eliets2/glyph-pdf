veraPDF — bundled third-party components
========================================

GlyphPDF bundles the veraPDF command-line validator and a minimal Java runtime
so that PDF/A conformance validation works out of the box. These are SEPARATE
programs, invoked by GlyphPDF only as a subprocess (never linked in-process).
This is license-safe aggregation; GlyphPDF itself remains under Apache-2.0.
