E-Signatures

Sign documents digitally with PAdES B-LT/B-LTA signatures (DSS/VRI embedding, TSA timestamps, a certify selector) and a visible ETSI-layout signature appearance with identity, date, reason and location lines that auto-fits the box. A Draw/Type/Upload/Initials picker places each signature as a real PDF annotation. On-page validity badges anchor to the actual signature fields — including in Two-Page mode — and Validate-All summarizes every signature's state. Protect > Sign > Prepare Request packages a document for other signers.

Status: parity scorecard §9.7 verified 9.0/10. Claims sourced from README.md, CHANGELOG.md and docs/audit/PARITY-SCORECARD-2026-09-30.md.

Tips

  • SHA-256 is the only signature hash; keys below 2048 bits are rejected before signing.
  • OCSP/CRL network access is consent-gated: a never-network policy still signs, and refusals are named honestly.
  • The session signature cache follows document switches, so a second placement reuses your signature.
