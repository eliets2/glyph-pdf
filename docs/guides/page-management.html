Page Management

The Pages surface handles document structure: rotate, crop, resize, reorder by dragging thumbnails on the grid, insert, extract, split by ranges into numbered part files (_part1, _part2, ...), and merge with real success/failure reporting. Headers, footers and page numbers can be stamped on, Bates numbering runs (including across documents in batch), and text or image watermarks apply with real font metrics.

Status: parity scorecard §9.9 verified 8.5/10. Claims sourced from README.md, CHANGELOG.md and docs/audit/PARITY-SCORECARD-2026-09-30.md.

  1. Step 1 — [to be captured from the redesigned UI: open the Pages mode and drag thumbnails to reorder; undo with Ctrl+Z (entry point to be confirmed from the redesigned UI)]
  2. Step 2 — [to be captured from the redesigned UI: rotate and crop a page; resize a page to a different paper size]
  3. Step 3 — [to be captured from the redesigned UI: split by ranges such as 1-3,4-6 and confirm the numbered part files are written]
  4. Step 4 — [to be captured from the redesigned UI: merge two documents and read the success/failure status honestly reported]
  5. Step 5 — [to be captured from the redesigned UI: add a header/footer with page numbers; then apply a Bates numbering run]
  6. Step 6 — [to be captured from the redesigned UI: apply a text watermark and an image watermark]

Tips

  • Reorder is one atomic permutation command — undo restores the whole arrangement at once.
  • Bates numbering continues across files in a batch run with truthful failure semantics (a fault at file two keeps file one committed).
  • Page Labels (Roman/section styles) have groundwork in place but the writer/UI is a named open item.
