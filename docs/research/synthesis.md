**Inputs:** 16 competitor spec sheets (acrobat, foxit, nitro, pdfxchange, masterpdf, updf, pdfexpert, pdfgear, sejda, bluebeam, ilovepdf, pdf24, libreoffice-draw, okular, abbyy-finereader, smallpdf) + 1 demand-mining report (r-pdf-mining.md), all in `C:\Users\User\Projects\pdf\.context\research\`; intersected with `C:\Users\User\Projects\pdf-parity\docs\audit\CURRENT-EVIDENCE-LEDGER-2026-09-05.md` (authoritative status) and `C:\Users\User\Projects\pdf-parity\PRD.md` (v1.3.1 §27/§28).

**Method.** (1) Every capability named in any report was tallied across the 16 tools (the "tool-demand" count = how many competitors ship it, which is itself the market's demand proof). (2) User-demand counts come from r/pdf thread frequencies in r-pdf-mining.md. (3) Each capability was intersected with the ledger: **LANDED** (ledger-verified, incl. F01–F12, U01–U08, V/N/D review fixes), **PARTIAL** (exists with named residual), **MISSING** (absent from ledger + PRD). (4) Rank = demand × offline-first fit × not-yet-built. (5) Confidence follows the research-specialist 6-level scale; citations are `file.md → section/row`. All claims below are graded by their source reports; nothing here upgrades a source's verdict.

**Ledger drift warning:** r-pdf-mining.md was written against the July audit baseline. ## 1. Executive Summary

The 16-tool corpus splits into three species: **subscription workstations** (Acrobat, Foxit, Nitro, ABBYY, Bluebeam — all five converted from perpetual to subscription between 2021–2025, all five carry documented licensing-anger scars), **cloud task grids** (Sejda, iLovePDF, Smallpdf, PDFgear, UPDF, PDF Expert — every one meters a free tier and uploads files by default), and **free/offline desktop tools** (PDF-XChange, Master PDF, PDF24, LibreOffice, Okular — the species GlyphPDF belongs to). The demand data (r/pdf) screams for exactly GlyphPDF's species: the four loudest themes are Acrobat enshittification anger (12+ threads), subscription anger with "one-time purchase" as the phrase-level demand (6+), fear of uploading confidential files (10+), and terror of fake redaction (17 threads — the largest theme in the corpus).

GlyphPDF's ledger shows it already owns an unusual concentration of what the market lacks: verified-excision redaction with transactional safety, PAdES B-LT/B-LTA signing, structural document compare, ensemble OCR with a verify screen, uncapped hot-folder batch + CLI, and local-only AI. The build-list that falls out is short and surgical: **four Tier-1 items** (measurement toolset — the only 8-of-16 tool-parity gap; redaction proof/verification UX — the corpus's #1 user demand that zero competitors serve; form-JavaScript execution; a local send-for-signing package), a Tier-2 batch of finish-the-parity packs (batch presets, find/replace + search depth, comment statuses + summary, accessibility authoring, cross-document Bates, dynamic stamps, DocMDP certify, OCG layers, auto-bookmarks, print presets), and Tier-3 niche plays. The anti-recommendation list is equally clear: every cloud-gated capability the subscription and task-grid species monetize — upload processing, cloud e-sign SaaS, cloud AI, DRM/remote-deactivation, ECM connectors — is a structural anti-feature that the demand data actively punishes.

- Offline fit: perfect — Qt overlay + PDFium geometry + measurement annots; no engine risk.
- Build note: Bluebeam's calibration-per-viewport and PDF-XChange's measurement-CSV export are the two features that make it professional-grade; ship those two first.

**T1-2. Redaction Proof Mode — post-apply self-verification + what-was-removed report + audit log.** *(Enters Tier 1 via the user-demand term: 17 r/pdf threads, the largest theme in the corpus; tool parity is 0/16 — nobody ships it, which makes it a differentiator rather than a parity item.)*

## 3. Anti-Recommendations (cloud-gated or demand-punished; do not build)

1. **Cloud processing / upload companion / "secure link" service.** The community's most repeated advice is *don't upload*; users DevTools-audit "no upload" claims (r-pdf-mining 4.2). It is also the documented ceiling of the entire task-grid species: Sejda's 3-tasks/hour wall (sejda.md F1), iLovePDF's caps that survive Premium (ilovepdf.md F3), Smallpdf's 2-tasks/day meter and vague logged-out retention (smallpdf.md F1). GlyphPDF's local-only stance is the product.
2. **Cloud AI chat / agents / AI-bloat marketing.** All 10 competitors that ship AI ship it cloud-gated (updf.md §14 "no AI capability is documented as offline"; pdfgear.md §14 Azure-OpenAI egress; smallpdf.md §14 metered; pdfexpert.md Copilot via OpenAI; foxit.md AI add-on). r/pdf's AI mentions are narrowly OCR accuracy and form detection — not assistants (r-pdf-mining anti #2). The local AI twin (Ollama lane, ledger F02/F03/D02) should exist as a quiet capability, not the identity; PDFgear's Copilot UX is the benchmark to copy at zero egress.
3. **Cloud e-sign SaaS / envelope backend.** Every competitor signing workflow is account-gated (see T1-4 evidence); ship the local package, not the service.
4. **Cloud document tracking / DRM / remote license deactivation.** Foxit's connectedPDF is discontinued — market validation that the category failed (foxit.md §1.14). Nitro deactivated pre-v13 perpetual licenses and earned BBB grade F (nitro.md B1–B3). "We cannot turn off your software" is a marketable sentence; never create the counter-example.
5. **ECM/DMS/SharePoint/iManage/OA connector suites.** Foxit's integration list is enormous maintenance for the wrong market (foxit.md §1.14 anti).
6. **Free-tier ambush mechanics: watermarks, nags, forced upgrades, feature-stripping.** Master PDF's v4→v5 watermark switch still breeds a 7-year-old fork package (masterpdf.md failure 1); Foxit's Reader nag/bloat tax (foxit.md failure 2); Readdle's PE7 paywalling (pdfexpert.md F1); UPDF's review-for-gift program and AI-on-top-of-lifetime upsell (updf.md failure 1/3).
7. **3D PDF authoring (U3D/PRC/BREP) and geospatial projection math.** Niche CAD workflows, enormous format/viewer cost; Acrobat, Foxit, PDF-XChange, Bluebeam all pay it (acrobat.md anti; foxit.md §1.1; pdfxchange.md §1; bluebeam.md §1).
8. **PDF Portfolios.** 1 r/pdf thread; Acrobat de-emphasizing (acrobat.md anti; r-pdf-mining anti #5) — while Foxit still invests (foxit.md §1.1; see conflict C10). Niche Acrobat-ism.
9. **Liquid-Mode-style cloud reflow; PostScript/Distiller ingestion; droplet .exe artifacts.** Acrobat anti list (acrobat.md): cloud AI reflow is anti; Distiller is a dead workflow for target users; supersede droplets with CLI + presets.
10. **Mobile-first / web-first investment now.** Browsers own casual viewing (r-pdf-mining 3.2); keep desktop the battleground.
11. **Admin cloud portals / telemetry analytics dashboards.** Nitro anti (nitro.md); the IT wedge is silent MSI + GPO/ADMX templates + offline license files instead (foxit.md rec #10, nitro.md rec #8).
12. **Gating shipped features behind accounts/tiers ("Smart Tools" pattern).** The exact mechanism that converted Nitro lovers into complainants (nitro.md anti); CapabilityRegistry discloses *why*, never upsells.
13. **Full print-production engine (separations, ink coverage, traps, PDF/X color management).** Nitro itself skips it (nitro.md anti); acrobat.md wants preflight-*lite* — the graded resolution is validation-and-fixups only, deferred color production (see conflict C12).
